# P31 — real application recordings

These clips are real browser recordings prepared for the AI Survival teaching animation (session date: 2026-11-18). They are prerecorded teaching simulations, not live patient measurements. The surrounding Word, Blender, PPTX and FFmpeg workflow is an HTML/SVG demonstration; it does not execute these tools on the audience’s computer. The finished preview is composed in HTML from the two recorded clips, not a claim that a new lesson MP4 has been exported.

- `cycling-v1.mp4` / `cycling-v1.webp`: [Cycling Energy Metabolism Simulator](https://www.kuaiyu.site/apps/cycling/), by Yu Kuai. Actual single-player entry challenge, operated with Space at a steady pace. No online race, AI report request, or user report was created.
- `heart-v1.mp4` / `heart-v1.webp`: [CardiacCycle 3D](https://www.kuaiyu.site/apps/cardiac_cycle/), by Yu Kuai. Actual 3D heart and synchronized Wiggers display. The clip and poster retain the heart rendering’s CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution.

For this slide: recorded the application, trimmed an eight-second segment, resized it to 960 × 540, encoded muted H.264 at 25 fps, and extracted its first frame as a WebP poster. The heart clip and poster, including these presentation modifications, are available under CC BY-SA 4.0. The slide resizes and arranges recordings in document, rendering, editing and preview scenes. No underlying anatomical meshes were modified for this slide.
